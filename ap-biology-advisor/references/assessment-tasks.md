# Current AP Biology Assessment Tasks

Read before generating or reviewing an exam-oriented item. The current contract
is the Fall 2025 CED with its June 2025 and June 2026 clarifications. Verify the
official exam page again before stating changeable administration facts.

## Shared rules

- Create original tasks. Do not reproduce secure AP Classroom, Progress Check,
  or Practice Exam content.
- Label synthetic investigations and data. Do not imply that a synthetic study
  was performed, published, or released by College Board.
- A stimulus must contain every fact, figure, table, model, and unit needed to
  answer the item. Keep dependent parts internally consistent.
- Use current Science Practice task verbs. Do not award a point for merely
  restating the prompt when the task requires explanation, evidence, reasoning,
  calculation, construction, or justification.
- A numeric score on a released response requires the official scoring guide
  for the same year, form, and question. Otherwise provide unscored feedback.
- Include an answer key, scoring notes, or worked response only when requested.
  Mark locally authored scoring notes as instructional, not official.

## Multiple choice

A current exam-oriented MCQ has four answer options and one best answer. It may
be discrete or belong to a set with a shared authentic-looking but synthetic
stimulus. Map the primary content Topic separately from the Practice needed to
resolve the item. Distractors should reflect plausible reasoning errors without
inventing misconceptions as scientific facts.

## Free response

Use `free-response-1` through `free-response-6` for the current task names
below. A component or partial task uses only the relevant Practices. A
`--full-task` validation must cover every required Practice-family group in
`ap-biology-boundaries.json`.

- **FRQ 1 — Interpreting and Evaluating Experimental Results (9 points):**
  authentic scenario plus data in a table, graph, or both; four parts cover
  concept description, methods and/or data description, methods/data analysis
  and/or calculation, and justified prediction.
- **FRQ 2 — Interpreting and Evaluating Experimental Results with Graphing
  (9 points):** authentic scenario plus tabular data; four parts cover concept
  description, construction of an appropriate graph, data/calculation/null
  hypothesis or result prediction, and justified prediction.
- **FRQ 3 — Scientific Investigation (4 points):** lab-investigation scenario;
  four parts cover concept description, procedure identification, null
  hypothesis or result prediction, and justification.
- **FRQ 4 — Conceptual Analysis (4 points):** authentic biological phenomenon
  with a disruption; four parts cover concept description, explanation,
  prediction of a cause or effect, and justification.
- **FRQ 5 — Analyze Model or Visual Representation of a Biological Concept or
  Process (4 points):** authentic scenario plus a visual model; four parts cover
  relevant concept description or application and analysis of visual
  characteristics and relationships, including a connection to a larger
  biological principle, concept, process, or theory. The task focuses on
  Practice 2 while also assessing Practice 1.
- **FRQ 6 — Analyze Data (4 points):** data in a graph, table, or other visual;
  four parts cover two data descriptions, evaluation of a hypothesis or
  prediction, and connection of results to a larger biological principle,
  concept, process, or theory.

For a graphing task, provide a blank grid or a representation the requested
medium can actually render. Scoring notes must check graph type, axes and units,
scale, plotted data, uncertainty when supplied, and trend line only when
appropriate. For calculations, show substitutions and units when the task
requires work; do not round away a conclusion-changing difference.
