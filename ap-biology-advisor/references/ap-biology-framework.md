# AP Biology — Internal Topic and Practice Catalog

This is a concise matching catalog for the current Fall 2025 framework, not an
official quotation or a copy of the CED. All listed Topics are assessed. Current
source versions, corrections, exclusions, and legacy markers live in
`ap-biology-boundaries.json`.

The validator's complete citation form is
`Unit <number>, Topic <code> — <title>`; for example,
`Unit 7, Topic 7.5 — Hardy–Weinberg Equilibrium`.

## Science Practices

- Practice 1 — Concept Explanation
  - 1.A Describe biological concepts and processes.
  - 1.B Explain biological concepts and processes.
  - 1.C Explain biological concepts and processes in applied contexts.
- Practice 2 — Visual Representations
  - 2.A Describe characteristics of visual representations of biological concepts and processes.
  - 2.B Explain relationships between characteristics of biological models in theoretical and applied contexts.
  - 2.C Explain how biological models relate to larger principles, concepts, processes, systems, or theories.
  - 2.D Represent relationships within biological models, including mathematical models, diagrams, flowcharts, and systems.
- Practice 3 — Questions and Methods
  - 3.A Identify or pose a testable question based on an observation, data, or a model.
  - 3.B State the null hypothesis or predict the results of an experiment.
  - 3.C Identify experimental procedures aligned with the question, including variables and appropriate controls.
  - 3.D Propose a new investigation based on an evaluation of experimental design or evidence.
- Practice 4 — Representing and Describing Data
  - 4.A Construct an appropriate, accurately plotted, scaled, and labeled graph.
  - 4.B Describe specific data, trends, patterns, and relationships in a table or graph.
- Practice 5 — Statistical Tests and Data Analysis
  - 5.A Perform calculations using curriculum equations, means, rates, ratios, percentages, and percent changes.
  - 5.B Use confidence intervals and error bars to estimate whether sample means are statistically different.
  - 5.C Perform chi-square hypothesis testing.
  - 5.D Use data to evaluate a hypothesis or prediction, including rejecting or failing to reject the null hypothesis.
- Practice 6 — Argumentation
  - 6.A Make a scientific claim.
  - 6.B Support a claim with evidence from biological principles, concepts, processes, and data.
  - 6.C Justify a claim by connecting evidence to biological theories.
  - 6.D Explain the relationship between experimental results and larger biological concepts, processes, or theories.
  - 6.E Predict the causes or effects of a change in, or disruption to, one or more components in a biological system.
